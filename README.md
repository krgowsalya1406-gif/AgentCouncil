## 🌌 About AgentCouncil

**What if one AI could think like an entire team of experts?**

That's the idea behind AgentCouncil.

AgentCouncil is an AI-powered multi-agent platform designed to bring different intelligent perspectives together. Instead of relying on a single AI response, the platform enables multiple specialized agents to examine a question, contribute their viewpoints, and work toward a more comprehensive answer.

Each agent approaches the problem from a different perspective, helping users explore ideas, evaluate possibilities, and make informed decisions.

### 🎯 Our Mission

To make AI-powered decision-making more intelligent, collaborative, and insightful through the power of multiple AI agents.
